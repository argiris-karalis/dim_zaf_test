About Me

Welcome

My name is Dimitris Zafeirelis; I am a professional guitarist based in Athens and one of the pioneers of the Greek jazz scene in the post-dictatorship era.

My journey began in the 1970s. Still a teenager, full of concerns—musical and otherwise—I found myself, along with other companions and colleagues, at the forefront of an exciting era. Together, we dared to experiment and establish a new genre of music that was groundbreaking for its time; a blend of different trends and movements that was destined to break new ground.

From those early days until now, I have been fortunate enough to collaborate with great Greek artists and to present my work at iconic art venues, festivals, and television shows in Greece and abroad.

Examples of Venues & Organizations:

ERT, the French Institute, the Hellenic-American Union, the British Council, "Olympia" (Paris), the Peace and Friendship Stadium, the "Ileana Tounta" Center for Contemporary Art, Skironios Museum, “Eumaros,” Aetopouleio Cultural Center of Halandri, DEPAK Kalamata.

Music Venues & Clubs:

"Mylos" and "Ydrogeios" (Thessaloniki), "Prova Club" (Ioannina), Jazzèt Cafè / Music Hall (Athens), Eumaros Book Cafe (Athens)

Festivals & Events:

Praxis Festival, "F. Nakas" Greek Jazz Festival, Thessaloniki & Corinth Jazz Festival, 1st Patras International Festival, Patras Rock Festival, Kavala & Chalkidiki International Ethnic Festival, Panhellenic Book Fair, Hellexpo, as well as cultural events organized by the Cultural Center of the Municipality of Athens.

My Main Gear 🎸

Fender Stratocaster (1979), Gibson Les Paul Standard, Kemper Profiler, Strymon Pedals.

Guitar Lessons

We offer private lessons (in classical, acoustic, and electric guitar) for all skill levels. Through a personalized program, we focus on your specific needs, whether you’re just starting your musical journey or want to delve deeper into advanced techniques.

🎸

Technique & Repertoire

Develop proper technique (picking, legato, fingerstyle) using a repertoire of classical, rock, jazz, and ethnic music, depending on the style that best expresses you.

🎼

Theory & Improvisation
